# Shared e-mobility pilot: Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh
Prepared with Green Plate by Team Green Plate (Hackathon 2026) · 10/10/2026

## Why here
- EV share of new vehicles in 2024: 16.6% (India 7.2%)
- Battery packs retiring in the district in 2030: about 2,560 t (744 t lithium-ion)
- Pilot zones chosen where the city lens and operator lens both score high

## Operator side
- Fleet: 205 vehicles; swap stations: 5 (1 per 50 vehicles)
- Rides a day: 1,025 (5 per vehicle) · revenue a month: ₹9.2 lakh at ₹30 a ride
- Upfront cost: ₹1.25 crore · payback: 45 months at 30% margin

## City side
- Parking bays: 21 · land needed: 415 m²
- Petrol saved: 683 litres a month · CO₂ avoided: 19 t a year
- Battery collection points needed across the district by 2030: 52 (50 t each)

## Who does what
- City: Parking bays and signage in each zone
- City: Single-window permit for the pilot area
- City: Land or rooftop space for swap stations
- City: Battery collection points near e-rickshaw stands
- Operator: Vehicles, app and on-ground team
- Operator: Swap stations and charging
- Operator: Battery take-back with supplier and authorised recycler
- Operator: Anonymised trip data to the city every month
- Together: Shared KPI dashboard and a 90-day review
- Together: Joint rider-safety and parking campaign

## First 90 days
- Week 1–2 (together): Agree the 5 pilot zones and sign a short MoU covering data sharing and battery take-back.
- Week 2–4 (city): Approve 21 parking bays and 100 m² for 5 swap stations; issue the pilot permit.
- Week 3–6 (operator): Install swap stations, deploy 205 vehicles, and register an authorised battery recycler.
- Week 6 (together): Soft launch in Z1 and Z2, then open the rest over two weeks.
- Day 90 (together): Review: target 1,025 rides a day, at least 90% of vehicles available, every retired battery logged. Decide to scale, adjust or stop.

## Assumptions
- Vehicles in a top zone: 60
- Rides per vehicle per day: 5
- Fare per ride (₹): 30
- Operating margin (0–1): 0.3
- Cost per vehicle (₹): 50000
- Vehicles per swap station: 50
- Cost per swap station (₹): 450000
- Vehicles per parking bay: 10
- Area per bay (m²): 15
- Land per swap station (m²): 20
- Average trip (km): 2.5
- Rides replacing petrol bikes (0–1): 0.4
- Petrol bike km per litre: 45
- Tonnes per collection point / year: 50

Data: VAHAN registrations by RTO (2001–2024), Prophet forecasts, district boundaries. Battery figures come from a cohort model with editable assumptions. Contact: team@example.com
